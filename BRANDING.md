Legitimate references to Trunks, including attribution, compatibility statements, and truthful descriptions of a fork, should remain possible.

When distributing a modified version, please make it clear that it is a fork, choose a name and visual identity that distinguish it from the official project, and do not imply official endorsement or affiliation. This document does not assign a new copyright license to existing artwork. These are potential third-party intellectual-property concerns, not findings about ownership or permission. The project owner should decide whether the concept image and name need review, replacement, or further clearance; no third-party rights are claimed here.
